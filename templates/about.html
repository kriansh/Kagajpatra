Sathi
साथी

Sathi is a bilingual guide that tells you exactly which documents to bring, which office to visit, and when it is open — for any Nepal government service. It runs a small on-device AI model so your questions never leave your phone.

{{ t.about_on_device }} AI
The assistant uses Gemma running locally via Ollama, or the Gemini API as a fallback. Compact quantized models keep it fast and private — your questions stay on your device.
{{ t.about_offline }}
Service worker caches all pages you have visited. Once loaded, Sathi works fully without an internet connection — essential for areas with poor connectivity.
Voice search
Ask in Nepali or English using your microphone. The voice layer is built as a clean adapter so a quantized Whisper model (via WebAssembly) can replace it later without changing any UI code.
Bilingual
All content and UI are available in English and Nepali (नेपाली). The language switch is always visible and remembered across sessions.
Document checklists
Every service has a tappable checklist. Tick items as you prepare. Your progress is saved on-device and restored when you return. You can copy, share, or print the list.
{{ t.about_open_source }}
Built for the open-source hackathon on tiny, edge-ready, on-device AI. Django 6 backend, plain CSS design tokens, no CDN runtime dependencies.

Office hours follow the Cabinet decision of 6 Apr 2026 (Rajpatra ref 26253): Mon–Fri 9 AM–5 PM, Sat+Sun weekly holidays. Always confirm with the office before you travel.
